# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Friends playing together online in rooms of 2–12 players.

## Product Purpose

«Нулевая смена» is a networked social-deduction game. Players move around a station, complete tasks, disrupt the crew, call meetings, and vote; the match ends with a crew or impostor victory.

## Positioning

The game combines first-person movement through a shared 3D station with social deduction, cooperative objectives, sabotage, meetings, and voting.

## Operating Context

Players create or join a room by code, prepare together in a walkable lobby, then play on desktop or touch devices. During a match, players navigate station rooms, interact with tasks and systems, communicate during meetings, and vote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current application includes networked rooms, player roles, tasks, sabotage, emergency and report meetings, chat, voting, a 3D station, player cosmetics, and desktop/mobile controls. Preserve the match loop and server-authoritative multiplayer behavior. Mobile controls and every primary interaction must remain usable without a desktop cursor. Avoid visually disruptive flicker.

## Brand Commitments

The user wants expressive, colorful cartoon character styling closer to the supplied «Gamble with Friends» reference, while keeping the characters and identity original rather than copying its characters or logo.

## Evidence on Hand

The current repository contains the game implementation and existing art assets. The supplied reference image is an aesthetic reference only; it does not grant rights to reproduce its characters or logo.

## Product Principles

- Make social deduction legible while the match is moving.
- Keep important actions reachable on touch screens.
- Let character silhouettes and color make players easy to distinguish.
- Make feedback clear without persistent flashing or visual noise.
